    <p>About 25,000 people live in Venice. There are beachfront homes on Venice Island. There is a historic downtown with brick streets that is easy to walk.     <h2>Remodeling &amp; New Construction in Venice</h2>
    <p>On Venice Island in particular, outside work can face design review. That is because the downtown and areas near the beach have historic charm. Homes further from the island, like those near Venice East, fall under more standard suburban zoning. They don't have the same downtown review. But they still must meet the same wind-load and flood elevation code. That code covers all of Sarasota County. We check which set of rules applies before we plan your job. Homes closer to the historic district also tend to have older electric and plumbing systems. These need to be checked before any remodel starts. Bringing them up to current code is often part of the job. That's true even when the main goal is the finish work you can see.</p>
    <h2>Storm &amp; Water Damage Restoration in Venice</h2>
    <p>On the island, the town faces the Gulf. So storm surge and wind-driven rain are real concerns for waterfront homes. Inland neighborhoods deal more with drainage. They also see roof damage from heavy rain in the rainy season. Either way, we check the damage, secure the home, and do the full rebuild. We do it all as one contractor who answers for the work.</p>
    <p>Most jobs inside Venice city limits go through the City of Venice's own building department. That is worth knowing. It runs on a separate timeline and process from the rest of Sarasota County.</p>
