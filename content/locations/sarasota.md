    <h2>Remodeling &amp; New Construction in Sarasota</h2>
    <p>Inside the City of Sarasota limits, jobs go through the city's building department. The areas around it, like parts near Lakewood Ranch, fall under Sarasota County instead. Then there is Siesta Key, right on the Gulf. It has extra coastal construction control line rules. These rules shape what you can add or build outside close to the beach. Downtown condo and townhome jobs also often need a condo or HOA board review. That comes on top of the normal city permit. It is good to bring it up early. It can add time that a single-family home job would not need.</p>
    <h2>Storm &amp; Water Damage Restoration in Sarasota</h2>
    <p>Downtown neighborhoods like Southside Village have more mixed lot sizes. They also have older homes than the newer areas further east. So we have more to check before we set a repair plan. We check how much power the electric system can handle. We check the shape the foundation is in. We check if past work had the right permits. Storm work on a downtown high-rise is not like work on a single-family home. The condo board and building managers are usually involved. They help plan access and the scope of work, along with the unit owner.</p>
    <p>On Siesta Key, beachfront repair work has one more step. It is the coastal construction control line review. Inland places in Sarasota don't face it. We build it into the timeline from the start.</p>
