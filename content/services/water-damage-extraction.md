        <h3>Sudden Events vs. Slow Leaks</h3>
        <p>Sudden events, like a burst pipe, are usually covered by insurance. A slow leak is not the same. Say it kept going for months and no one fixed it. That damage is often treated as an upkeep issue, and it may not be covered. If you see a slow leak, fix it fast. That protects both your home and your claim. Watch for a small water stain that keeps coming back after you clean it. It is one of the more common early signs of a slow leak. Check it out before it turns into a bigger claim.</p>
