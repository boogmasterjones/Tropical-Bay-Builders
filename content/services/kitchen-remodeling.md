        <h3>How Long Does a Kitchen Remodel Take?</h3>
        <p>Most full kitchen remodels take four to eight weeks. It depends on the size of the job, wait times for materials, and layout changes. A cabinet and countertop refresh goes faster. A full gut remodel that moves plumbing and electric takes longer. Your estimate will give you a timeline for your own project, not a general range. Wait times for materials can shift that window too. Custom cabinets often take longer to order and arrive than the building work itself. So we build that into your schedule up front. That way it does not surprise you partway through.</p>
        <div class="callout"><p>"Budget is usually the first thing people ask about. That is a fair question. We walk every kitchen in person before we give a number. A phone estimate on a job like this is rarely close enough to plan around."</p></div>
        <h3>What Drives the Cost of a Kitchen Remodel</h3>
        <p>Cabinets and countertop material are usually the biggest costs. Next is whether the layout changes or stays in place. Changing it means moving plumbing and electric. Custom cabinets cost more than semi-custom. Quartz or granite countertops cost more than laminate. But they also hold up better to Florida's humid air over time. We talk through these choices with you before you commit to anything. New appliances, lights, and backsplash tile cost less one by one. But they add up. We list each one on its own line in your estimate. That way you can see just where the budget is going. You can change it before work starts, not after.</p>
