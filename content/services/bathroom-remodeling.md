        <h3>Tub-to-Shower Conversions</h3>
        <p>Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most asked-for bathroom jobs. We handle the plumbing, waterproofing, and tile work as one job. Planning ahead to age in place?         <h3>Stopping Moisture &amp; Mold</h3>
        <p>We use the right waterproofing layers. Our tile slopes the right way to the drain. We add enough venting, too. These details matter more in Florida weather than almost anywhere else. Skip any one of these, and a bathroom remodel usually fails within a few years. Done right, it lasts a decade or more. Exhaust fans are also often too small for the room. That sounds minor. But it is one of the most common reasons for mildew in a well-built bathroom. The mildew shows up along the ceiling line within a year or two.</p>
