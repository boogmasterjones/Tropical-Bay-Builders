        <h3>How Long Does Epoxy Flooring Last?</h3>
        <p>Prepped and put down right, an epoxy floor can last many years with normal use. Good surface prep is the biggest factor in how long it lasts. The concrete must be ground the right way to open its surface pores. Any cracks or moisture problems must be fixed. And the slab needs enough time to cure. We grind instead of acid-etching before coating. Machine grinding makes a more even surface than a chemical etch alone. That gives the epoxy a better surface to bond to.</p>
        <div class="callout"><p>"Skipping surface prep is why some epoxy jobs peel or bubble within a year or two. It is the step we spend the most time on, no matter how simple the finish coat looks."</p></div>
        <h3>Decorative Flake vs. Solid Color</h3>
        <p>We offer solid-color and decorative flake epoxy. Pick based on your look and budget. Flake finishes tend to hide small flaws and daily wear better than solid color. That is why they are popular for garage floors that get heavy use. Solid color gives a cleaner, more even look. Some homeowners like it for workshops or showroom-style spaces. In those spaces, a polished look matters more than hiding wear.</p>
