        <h3>Insurance Discounts for Wind Mitigation</h3>
        <p>A wind mitigation inspection records what your home has now. It also shows what would qualify if you upgraded. Do it before you decide what comes first, not after. Many homeowners are surprised by how much these upgrades can save. Over time, they can offset insurance costs. The inspection itself is fairly quick. Having it on file gives you a clear starting point. You can work from it as you decide what to do first.</p>

        <h3>Picking What Comes First on a Budget</h3>
        <p>You may not be able to do it all before the next storm season. If so, we will help you pick what comes first. We base it on the weak spots of your own home, not a one-size-fits-all list. A home near open water needs different things than one farther inland. Impact windows and doors are often next after the roof. High winds can punch a hole in the shell of the home. That is one of the most common ways damage inside gets worse fast. Garage doors are often left out of storm plans, too. But a garage door can fail in the wind. Then pressure can build up in the whole building. That can cause damage far past the garage itself.</p>
