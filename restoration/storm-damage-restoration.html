Avoiding Post-Storm Scams

Legitimate contractors are usually swamped with existing calls in the days right after a major weather event, and high-pressure, same-day sign-up pitches from unfamiliar names are one of the most common scams that follow Florida storms. Verify licensing before anyone gets on your roof. Asking for a physical business address and checking that the license is active with the state takes only a few minutes and can save you from a much larger headache.
